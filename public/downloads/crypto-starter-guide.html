⚠️ Disclaimer: Educational content only. Not financial advice. Never invest money you can't afford to lose.

Blockchain basics (plain English)

  • Blockchain — a public digital ledger shared across thousands of computers. No single entity controls it.
  • Coin vs token — coins have their own blockchain (Bitcoin, Solana); tokens run on another chain (most meme coins are Solana/ETH tokens).
  • Wallet — your key to the chain. Holds your private keys, not the coins themselves.
  • Gas/network fees — small costs paid to process transactions. Solana fees are fractions of a cent; ETH can be higher.
  • Exchange — where you buy/sell crypto (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, or DEXs like Jupiter/Raydium).

Set up your first wallet (step by step)

  1. Pick a self-custody wallet: Phantom (Solana), MetaMask (Ethereum) — or a hardware wallet for larger amounts (Ledger)
  2. Download only from the official site/App Store (fake wallets are the #1 scam)
  3. Write down your 12-24 word seed phrase on PAPER. Never screenshot it, never type it online
  4. Store the paper somewhere safe (fireproof box, bank deposit box)
  5. Never share your seed phrase with anyone — support will NEVER ask for it

DogeCoin deep dive — history, utility, community

  • History: Created 2013 as a joke (Shiba Inu meme). Turned into one of the most recognized cryptocurrencies in the world.
  • Utility: Low-fee payments, tipping, charitable fundraising. Accepted by some merchants.
  • Community: One of the largest, most loyal communities in crypto. Doge culture = fun, memes, and "much wow" energy.
  • Meme coin reality check: Meme coins are high-risk, high-volatility. Never invest more than you can lose. DYOR always.

Security checklist

  • ☐ Seed phrase on paper, offline, never shared
  • ☐ 2FA enabled on exchange accounts (authenticator app, not SMS)
  • ☐ Wallet downloaded from official source only
  • ☐ Small test transaction before large transfers
  • ☐ Double-check addresses before sending (copy-paste + verify first/last chars)
  • ☐ Beware DMs claiming to be "support" — always scams
  • ☐ No "giveaway" links, ever
