Staking Solana is one of the easiest ways to earn passive income in crypto. Current APY rates range from 6-8% with minimal effort. Here is everything you need to know to start staking SOL in 2026.

How Staking Works

When you stake SOL, you delegate your tokens to a validator who processes transactions on the Solana network. In exchange, you receive a portion of the network inflation rewards and transaction fees. Your SOL never leaves your wallet. You maintain full ownership and can unstake at any time (with a 1-3 day cooldown period). Staking rewards are distributed automatically every epoch (approximately every 2 days).

Choosing a Validator

Look for validators with: 95%+ uptime (reliability), commission under 10% (your cut), and a reputable operator (check their social presence and history). Avoid validators with 0% commission — this is often a short-term tactic to attract delegations before raising rates. Diversify across 2-3 validators to reduce risk. Popular options include Laine, Coinbase Cloud, Figment, and Everstake.

Getting Started

Buy SOL on an exchange. Transfer to Phantom or Solflare wallet. Click "Stake" in your wallet. Select a validator. Confirm the transaction. Done. Your rewards will start accumulating immediately. Track your rewards and APY monthly. Re-delegate if a validator's performance drops.
