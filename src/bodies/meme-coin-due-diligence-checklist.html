How to Spot Scams, Rugs, and Honeypots Before You Lose Your SOL

The Solana meme coin gold rush has attracted incredible communities — but also bad actors. In 2024–2025 alone, over $400M was lost to Solana rug pulls and honeypot scams. Use this checklist before every meme coin purchase to protect your portfolio. Score each coin and never buy anything below 7/10.

🔍 Step 1: Token Contract Analysis

Every legitimate meme coin has its contract verified on Solscan. Never buy a coin you can't verify.

  • Contract verified? Check on Solscan.io or Birdeye. Verified = green checkmark next to contract.
  • Mint authority disabled? If mint authority is still active, devs can mint infinite tokens. This is the #1 red flag.
  • Freeze authority disabled? If active, devs can freeze your tokens. Only acceptable for regulated tokens.
  • LP tokens burned or locked? Check if liquidity pool tokens were burned (sent to dead wallet). If not locked, devs can pull liquidity at any time.
  • Top 10 holders % < 30%? High concentration = risk of dump. Anything above 50% is a major red flag.
🚩 RED FLAG: Mint authority still active. If the devs can print more tokens, they can dilute your bags into nothing. Most legit projects renounce mint authority within days of launch.

👥 Step 2: Team & Community Analysis

  • Team doxxed? At minimum: anonymous team with consistent track record (previous successful projects).
  • Telegram/Discord members > 5,000 real? Check member quality — are accounts posting real messages or just bots?
  • Social channels active for > 30 days? Rug pulls often have social accounts created days before launch.
  • GitHub activity? Even meme coins should have some public code or at least a website with a development roadmap.
  • No "Get Rich Quick" promises? Legitimate communities focus on culture and fun, not guaranteed returns.
🚩 RED FLAG: Devs use a brand-new Twitter account with no history, or the Telegram is "muted" so only admins can speak. Community should be able to ask questions freely.

📊 Step 3: Liquidity & Trading Analysis

  • Liquidity > $50,000? On Solana, anything below $10k LP is extremely risky for slippage and rug pulls.
  • Daily volume > $10k? Legitimate coins have organic trading volume. Zero volume = dead project.
  • Slippage test < 5%? Try buying a small amount (0.1 SOL). If slippage is >10%, liquidity is too thin.
  • No buy/sell tax > 10%? Some tax is normal (2–5% for marketing). Anything above 10% may be a trap.
  • Can you sell? Test with a tiny purchase first. Honeypots let you buy but not sell. Always test both directions.
🚩 RED FLAG: You can buy but the transaction reverts when you try to sell. This is a honeypot. Use a tiny test transaction (0.01 SOL) before buying in any size.

🌐 Step 4: Website & Branding Analysis

  • Website looks professional? Does it load properly? Are there broken links or obvious template errors?
  • Roadmap realistic? Does the project have achievable goals or is it promising "10,000x returns"?
  • Whitepaper or litepaper? Even a one-page document explaining the project is a good sign.
  • Social links all work? Twitter, Telegram, Discord, Medium — check they're all live and active.
  • Brand consistency? Same logos, color schemes, and messaging across all platforms.
✅ GREEN FLAG: The website has been around for more than 3 months, has active blog/content updates, and provides clear tokenomics on a dedicated page.

📈 Step 5: On-Chain Metrics (Advanced)

Use Birdeye, DexScreener, or Solscan for these advanced checks:

  • Holder growth trend — Increasing daily? Flat or declining holders is a warning sign.
  • New vs. existing holder ratio — Healthy projects have a steady stream of new holders, not just existing whales accumulating more.
  • Whale wallet concentration — Check if any single wallet holds more than 5% of supply. One whale can dump on the whole community.
  • Transaction history patterns — Look for wash trading (same wallets trading back and forth to fake volume).
  • Age of oldest holders — Long-term holders who haven't sold are a strong bullish signal.
🚩 RED FLAG: The same 5–10 wallets account for 80%+ of daily trading volume. This is almost certainly wash trading to create fake activity and lure in buyers.
