Stablecoins are cryptocurrencies designed to maintain a stable value, usually pegged to the US dollar. They are essential for trading, DeFi, and avoiding crypto volatility. Here is how the major stablecoins work on Solana.

USDC (USD Coin)

USDC is the most trusted stablecoin on Solana. Issued by Circle, fully backed by US dollars and short-term US Treasuries. Regular third-party audits confirm the backing. USDC on Solana is the fastest and cheapest way to move US dollars globally. Transactions cost fractions of a cent and settle in seconds. Most Solana DEXes use USDC as their primary trading pair.

USDT (Tether)

USDT is the largest stablecoin by market capitalization. It is widely used across all blockchains including Solana. Tether's backing is more controversial than USDC's, but it remains the most liquid stablecoin on many exchanges. USDT on Solana offers the same speed and low fees as USDC.

DAI (MakerDAO)

DAI is a decentralized stablecoin not controlled by any company. It is backed by crypto collateral (over-collateralized loans on Maker protocol). DAI is less liquid than USDC and USDT on Solana but offers censorship resistance. Useful for users who want to avoid centralized stablecoin issuers.
