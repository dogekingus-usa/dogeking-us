What is DeFi Yield Farming?

Yield farming is the process of earning rewards by providing liquidity to decentralized finance protocols. On Solana, it's fast, low-cost, and accessible to beginners.

Getting Started

Step 1: Set up a Phantom or Solflare wallet.
Step 2: Fund your wallet with SOL (for gas fees) and the tokens you want to farm.
Step 3: Choose your protocol: Jupiter (best aggregator), Raydium (largest DEX), Orca (user-friendly), Kamino (automated strategies), Marginfi (lending).

Top Yield Farming Strategies for 2026

1. Stablecoin Lending (8-15% APY) — Safest
Deposit USDC/USDT on Marginfi or Kamino. Low risk, consistent returns. No impermanent loss.

2. SOL Staking (7-9% APY) — Very Safe
Stake SOL via Phantom wallet or Jito. Support network security while earning yield.

3. Concentrated Liquidity Pools (20-60% APY) — Medium Risk
Provide liquidity on Orca or Raydium for major pairs like SOL/USDC. Higher yield but risk of impermanent loss.

4. Leveraged Yield Farming (50-150% APY) — High Risk
Use Kamino or Marginfi to borrow against deposits and amplify yield. Only for experienced users.

Risk Management

Never farm with more than 30% of your portfolio. Diversify across 3-5 protocols. Check smart contract audits. Start with small amounts to learn the mechanics. Monitor positions weekly.
