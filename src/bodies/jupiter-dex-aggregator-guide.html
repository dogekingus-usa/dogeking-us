Jupiter is the most popular DEX aggregator on Solana. It finds the best swap routes across all Solana DEXes, saving you money on every trade. Here is how to use it effectively in 2026.

Basic Swaps

Go to jup.ag. Connect your Phantom or Solflare wallet. Select the token you want to swap from and the token you want to receive. Jupiter automatically finds the best route across all Solana DEXes. Review the swap details including price impact, fees, and minimum received. Confirm the transaction in your wallet. Jupiter's routing saves 1-3% per trade compared to using a single DEX.

Limit Orders

Jupiter offers limit orders — set a target price and the swap executes automatically when the market reaches that price. Useful for buying dips without watching charts all day. Set your target price, amount, and expiration. Jupiter monitors the market and executes when your conditions are met. No gas fees for setting up limit orders.

DCA (Dollar Cost Average)

Jupiter's DCA feature lets you automatically buy a fixed amount of a token at regular intervals. Set your total investment, interval (hourly, daily, weekly), and duration. Jupiter executes the purchases automatically. DCA reduces the risk of buying at market peaks and is the recommended strategy for long-term accumulation.
